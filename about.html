About Us

Sixty years of brass, three generations of craft.

Ranjit Industries was founded in 1964 in Jamnagar — the brass capital of India — as a small family workshop turning brass parts for local electricians. Six decades on, the workshop has grown into a modern manufacturing facility supplying precision components to customers in Germany, the United Kingdom, Italy, France, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and beyond.

Our story

The founder, Shri Ranjit Bhai, learned turning from his father on hand-operated lathes. In 1964, with three machines and four craftsmen, he began supplying electrical accessory parts to workshops across Gujarat. His son formalised the business and introduced CNC turning in the 1990s. Today the third generation runs the firm, combining sliding-head CNC, in-house hot forging, and full plating under one roof.

What has not changed is the discipline. Every part is inspected against the drawing before it leaves. Every material heat is traceable to a mill certificate. Every commitment is honoured.

Our principles

Draw once, deliver forever. We tool up carefully so repeat orders match the first shipment.

Say the honest date. Lead times are quoted from confirmed capacity, not from optimism. When we say four weeks, we mean four weeks.

The right brass for the job. We work in CW614N, CW617N and lead-free CW724R depending on the application — machining quality, forgeability, or drinking-water compliance.

Certifications

ISO 9001 : 2015 quality management. RoHS and REACH compliance on request. Lead-free CW724R parts certified for potable water contact to relevant European standards.
