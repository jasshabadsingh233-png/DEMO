    Playbook
    How to actually time an LME purchase
    1. Anchor to the 200-day moving average. A price 5–10% below the 200-day, with RSI < 35, is historically the strongest single entry signal for base metals.
    2. Watch inventory + spread together. Falling LME stocks and a backwardated cash-3M spread ($ > 0) means physical is genuinely tight — buy now, don't try to pick the bottom.
    3. Split your buys. Even with a strong Buy signal, ladder in: 40% today, 30% at –3%, 30% at –6%. Never single-shot the annual requirement.
    4. Hedge what you can't take. If you're quoted on a fixed price 90 days out but can only physically take delivery in 180, buy the 3M contract and roll — it costs less than a 5% price move.
    5. Use the DXY as an early warning. A DXY breakout above 106 has preceded ~80% of base-metal drawdowns since 2010. When it moves, tighten your buying window.
    6. Don't fight PMI. Chinese manufacturing PMI < 49 for two consecutive months = wait; > 51 for two = accumulate.
