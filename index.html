Capabilities

Four workshops, one gate to gate.

Every component leaves our floor fully finished, inspected and export-packed.

CNC Turning

Sliding-head and chucker machines producing turned parts from 3 mm to 60 mm diameter, in tolerances to ±0.02 mm.

Hot Forging

Six presses from 100 T to 500 T. Complex shapes in CW617N for sanitary, gas and heating fittings.

Plating & Finishing

Nickel, chrome, tin and zinc plating in-house. Polishing, tumbling and passivation to your finish specification.

Quality & Compliance

ISO 9001 certified. RoHS, REACH and drinking-water compliance. Material and dimensional reports with every shipment.
