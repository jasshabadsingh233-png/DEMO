    Solar Advance Machinery Limited

    Founded 2025 · Electronics & Sensor Integration Startup

    Mission

    Bring intelligence to industrial fluid systems — bridging the gap between commodity fluid fittings and IoT-enabled monitoring systems that require no batteries or external wiring.

    The Insight

    Tier-1 manufacturers — semiconductor fabs, solar and battery-storage OEMs, industrial utilities — are losing visibility into their coolant loops, process utility lines, and thermal management systems.

    Volturn V1

    A self-powered smart brass fitting that harvests energy from fluid pressure itself and broadcasts real-time conditions over Bluetooth. No battery. No external connector. Drop-in replacement for standard fittings.

    Status

    Pre-prototype stage. Validating the energy harvester and wireless architecture.

    Target Markets

    Semiconductor fabs · Solar/battery storage OEMs · Industrial utilities · Thermal management systems.
